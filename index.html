Meet the maker

Every stitch is made by hand.

Yube Handmade is a crochet fashion label dedicated to creating stylish, high quality crochet pieces. It began as a personal hobby, a way to unwind and express creativity, and grew into a label for people who want something made with care.

Because every piece is crocheted by hand and often made to order, each one carries small, one of a kind details you will not find anywhere else.
